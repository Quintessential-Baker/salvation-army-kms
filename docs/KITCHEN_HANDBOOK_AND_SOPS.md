# The Salvation Army — Butler, PA Kitchen & Dining Room SOPs

## Schedule & Service Hours
- **Lunch Service Days:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday (11:30 AM – 12:30 PM)
- **Standard Shift:** 8:30 AM – 2:30 PM
- **Admin, Pantry Intake & Deep Prep Day:** Thursday (No public hot lunch service)

## Core ServSafe & Storage Protocols
- **Day-Dot Labeling:** All prepped items, leftovers, and opened commercial packages must be transferred out of metal cans into food-safe containers and labeled with **Item Name, Prep/Open Date, Use-By Date (max 7 days at ≤ 41°F), and Initials**.
- **Temperature Critical Limits:** Cold holding `≤ 41°F` | Hot holding `≥ 135°F` | Poultry & reheated leftovers `≥ 165°F`.
- **2-Stage Cooling:** Cool hot foods from `135°F` to `70°F` within **2 hours**, and from `70°F` to `41°F` or below within **4 additional hours** (6 hours total).
- **Inventory & Ordering Cycle:** Log daily usage and spoilage/breakage ("crack") immediately; conduct full 6-zone blind physical counts on Fridays; submit consolidated monthly vendor orders by the **3rd Friday of each month**.
